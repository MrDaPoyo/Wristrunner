# Wristrunner

## Features
- Pogo pins on the back for extensions (1x 5V, 1x GND, and i2c maybe)
- Small and slim. 80 mm x 165 mm x 15mm
- A display with as small as possible bezels.
- Battery should last at least 5 hours.
- At least 4GBs of RAM.
- Back: Pogo pins for extensions.
- Side: A handlebar / rail to mount the extensions.

## Internals
- SoC: Rockchip RK3576 https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C42388007.html
- RAM: 8GBs of LPDDR4X https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C46681826.html
- Cooling: Passively-cooled by the aluminum chassis/casing.
- Display: http://aliexpress.com/item/1005008854283267.html (MIPI)
- Rotating camera: MIPI CSI 2 Camera w/ Micro Servo
- Wifi: Integrated
